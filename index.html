· The Ritual ·

Your comforting bowl in three steps.

01

Open a sachet

Empty one 16 g sachet into a saucepan. No scooping, no measuring, no half-used bag going stale.

02

Add 200 ml milk

Whole milk, or water if you prefer. Stir once so the barley doesn’t catch on the base.

03

Heat, stir & serve

Five to seven minutes on a low flame, stirring occasionally. Top with dates, honey, or nothing at all.
